ASHLINE

A round-based tactical shooter in Kestrel Yard, a dusk freight yard of containers and crane steel. Five a side against bot squads. Win the round by eliminating the other team — or, if your team attacks, by planting the charge at the Crane Yard or the Depot and holding until it blows. Defenders can defuse it. Kills and rounds pay; spend the money in the buy phase on rifles, kevlar and grenades. Accuracy beats speed: stop moving to shoot straight. First to seven rounds, sides swap at the half.
